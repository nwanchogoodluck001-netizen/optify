Understanding Your Transactions

Every balance movement is recorded in your transaction history.

Earnings

Rewards from Streak, Mystery Box, Quiz and eligible referrals.

Payments

Dashboard validation and Withdrawal E-pin payments.

Withdrawals

Withdrawal requests and any corresponding reversals.

Transaction Rules

Important information about your transaction history.

  • Every balance-changing event has a corresponding transaction.
  • Completed rewards are recorded only after backend verification.
  • Withdrawals may remain pending while they are being processed.
  • Rejected withdrawals may create a reversal transaction.
  • Users cannot edit transaction records.
  • Duplicate requests must not create duplicate financial records.

Your Transaction History Is Protected

Keep your account information private.

Transaction ownership, amounts, references, statuses and balance changes are controlled by the backend. Never share passwords, payment references or sensitive account details with another person.
