How the Referral Program Works

Your referral reward follows a simple four-step process.

STEP 01

Share

Share your personal Optify referral link with someone you know.

STEP 02

Join

Your friend registers through your referral link.

STEP 03

Validate

Your referred user completes their dashboard validation.

STEP 04

Earn

Once eligible, the $5 referral reward can be credited to your account.

Important Referral Rule

A registration by itself does not qualify as a rewarded referral. The referred user must successfully complete the required dashboard validation before the referral can become eligible for the $5 reward.
